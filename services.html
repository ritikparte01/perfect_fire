Our Services

FIRE EXTINGUISHER SERVICE.

At PERFECT FIRE & SAFETY SERVICES , we understand the importance of early fire detection. Our team specializes in the installation and maintenance of state-of-the-art fire alarm systems. Equipped with smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual pull stations, and alarm notification devices, these systems provide timely alerts to occupants, enabling safe evacuation and swift response from fire authorities.

FIRE HYDRANT SYSTEM.

Our expertise lies in the installation, maintenance, and inspection of fire hydrant systems. These systems consist of underground water pipelines connected to above-ground hydrants, ensuring a ready supply of water for firefighting purposes. By meticulously designing and maintaining these networks of pipes, valves, and water sources, we ensure a swift response in extinguishing fires effectively.

CONTRACTS

Annual Maintenance Contracts and Fire Extinguisher Service We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) to ensure the continued reliability and longevity of fire safety equipment and systems. Our dedicated technicians conduct regular inspections, servicing, and maintenance, keeping the equipment in proper working condition and compliant with safety standards. Additionally, our fire extinguisher service includes refilling, pressure testing, and general maintenance to ensure that these portable devices are ready for use during fire emergencies.

We conduct interactive fire and safety awareness demos to educate individuals, employees, and communities on fire safety measures, emergency evacuation plans, and best practices for preventing fire hazards.
