About Us

Introduction:
At PERFECT FIRE & SAFETY SERVICES , we are dedicated to promoting fire safety and protecting lives and property from fire-related risks. With our comprehensive range of services, we strive to provide reliable solutions for fire protection and emergency preparedness.
Fire Hydrant System:
Our expertise lies in the installation, maintenance, and inspection of fire hydrant systems. These systems consist of underground water pipelines connected to above-ground hydrants, ensuring a ready supply of water for firefighting purposes. By meticulously designing and maintaining these networks of pipes, valves, and water sources, we ensure a swift response in extinguishing fires effectively.
Fire Alarm System:
At PERFECT FIRE & SAFETY SERVICES , we understand the importance of early fire detection. Our team specializes in the installation and maintenance of state-of-the-art fire alarm systems. Equipped with smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual pull stations, and alarm notification devices, these systems provide timely alerts to occupants, enabling safe evacuation and swift response from fire authorities.
Annual Maintenance Contracts and Fire Extinguisher Service:
Annual Maintenance Contracts and Fire Extinguisher Service We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) to ensure the continued reliability and longevity of fire safety equipment and systems. Our dedicated technicians conduct regular inspections, servicing, and maintenance, keeping the equipment in proper working condition and compliant with safety standards. Additionally, our fire extinguisher service includes refilling, pressure testing, and general maintenance to ensure that these portable devices are ready for use during fire emergencies.
Fire and Safety Awareness Demos:
Fire and Safety Awareness Demos Education and awareness are at the core of our mission. We conduct interactive fire and safety awareness demos to educate individuals, employees, and communities on fire safety measures, emergency evacuation plans, and best practices for preventing fire hazards. Through practical demonstrations, interactive sessions, and informative materials, we aim to empower people with the knowledge and skills to prevent, respond to, and mitigate fire-related incidents.

Our Quality Services

Hydrant System Installation & AMC

We provide complete fire hydrant system design, installation, testing, and annual maintenance as per IS standards. Our team ensures proper pressure, pump performance, and safety compliance for commercial, residential, and industrial projects across Vasai–Virar, Palghar & Mumbai.

Sprinkler System Installation & AMC

From residential buildings to large industrial facilities, we install automatic sprinkler systems designed to control and suppress fire quickly. Regular AMC ensures your system stays reliable and compliant with the latest fire safety norms.

Fire Extinguisher – New Supply, Refilling & AMC

We supply ISI-certified fire extinguishers, including ABC, CO₂, DCP, Foam, and Water types. Our services include refilling, recharging, hydro-testing, and periodic maintenance, ensuring your fire extinguishers are always ready during emergencies.

Fire Tubing / Gas Suppression System

We provide advanced automatic fire detection tubing and gas suppression systems ideal for server rooms, electrical panels, data centers, and enclosed spaces. These systems activate instantly to suppress fire without damaging equipment.

Fire Safety Training & Mock Drills

Our experts conduct on-site fire safety training, mock drills, evacuation planning, and extinguisher handling workshops for offices, schools, societies, hospitals, and industries. We help your team stay prepared and confident in real emergencies.

Survey Reports

We carry out detailed fire safety surveys and site inspections to assess risks, system health, equipment condition, and compliance with IS/BIS standards. A full survey report helps you upgrade safety measures and meet fire department requirements.

CCTV Camera Installation & Servicing

Enhance security with our professional CCTV installation, configuration, and maintenance services. We offer HD/IP camera setups for homes, societies, commercial spaces, warehouses, and industries.
