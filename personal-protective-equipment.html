🦺 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Perfect Fire & Safety Services provides a comprehensive range of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and safety gear across Mumbai, Palghar, Vasai-Virar, and All Over Maharashtra. Our PPE solutions are designed to protect workers from fire, chemical, electrical, and physical hazards in industrial and commercial environments.

From basic safety helmets to advanced breathing apparatus, we supply high-quality protective equipment that meets international safety standards (CE, NIOSH, ISI). Our PPE range ensures complete protection for workers in construction, manufacturing, firefighting, chemical processing, and other hazardous occupations.

👷‍♂️ Head & Face Protection

  • Safety Helmets: HDPE/ABS hard hats with chin straps
  • Bump Caps: Lightweight head protection
  • Face Shields: Full face protection from sparks/chemicals
  • Safety Goggles: Impact and chemical resistant
  • Welding Helmets: Arc flash and radiation protection
  • Hearing Protection: Earplugs and noise-canceling headsets

🫁 Respiratory Protection

  • Self Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA): 30-45 minute emergency air supply
  • Air Line Breathing Apparatus (ALBA): Continuous air supply systems
  • Escape Breathing Apparatus (EBA): 10-15 minute emergency escape
  • Full Face Masks: P3/ABEK filter compatibility
  • Half Face Respirators: Chemical and particle filtration
  • Disposable Masks: N95, P2, FFP2/FFP3 rated

🔥 Fire & Heat Protection

  • Fire Proximity Suits: Aluminized heat-reflective clothing
  • Fire Entry Suits: Complete body protection for firefighting
  • Flame Resistant Coveralls: Nomex/Kevlar materials
  • Heat Resistant Gloves: Up to 1000°C temperature protection
  • Fire Blankets: Personal emergency protection
  • Flash Hood: Head and neck protection from flash fires

🏗️ Fall Protection & Safety Harness

  • Full Body Harness: 5-point adjustment with dorsal D-ring
  • Safety Lanyards: Shock-absorbing with snap hooks
  • Retractable Lifelines: 3-30 meter self-retracting
  • Rope Access Equipment: Climbing and positioning gear
  • Safety Nets: Perimeter and debris protection
  • Anchor Points: Permanent and temporary attachment

🧤 Hand & Body Protection

  • Chemical Resistant Gloves: Nitrile, PVC, neoprene materials
  • Cut Resistant Gloves: HPPE fibers with PU coating
  • Electrical Safety Gloves: Insulated up to 36kV
  • Disposable Gloves: Latex, nitrile, vinyl options
  • Safety Coveralls: Chemical and biological protection
  • High-Vis Clothing: Class 2/3 reflective garments

👢 Foot Protection

  • Safety Shoes: Steel toe caps, slip-resistant soles
  • Gumboots: Chemical and water resistant
  • Electrical Safety Boots: Insulated for electrical work
  • Fire Resistant Boots: Heat and flame protection
  • Anti-Static Footwear: ESD protection in electronics

🏢 Ideal For

  • Construction sites, manufacturing plants, chemical industries, oil refineries, power plants, mining operations, and emergency response teams.
